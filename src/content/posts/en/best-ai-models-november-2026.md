## How these rankings were built

Every ranking blends three kinds of evidence, in this order of weight: independent benchmarks first, the labs' own launch numbers second, and what real users report third.

- **Independent leaderboards.** [Artificial Analysis](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys) (Intelligence Index v5.0 and its runs of Terminal-Bench 4.0, GDPval-AA, AutomationBench, AA-Omniscience, Humanity's Last Exam, Harvey LAB-AA, the Cyber Index and CWE-Bench), [Arena](https://x.com/arena) (Text, WebDev, Vision, Agent and Image-to-WebDev), plus Vals, Scale's MCP Atlas and APEX-Agents. I read most of them through BenchLeader, which mirrors those boards daily.
- **Lab launch tables.** [OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI), [Anthropic](https://x.com/AnthropicAI), [Google DeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind), [Moonshot](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot), [DeepSeek](https://x.com/deepseek_ai), [Zhipu](https://x.com/Zai_org), [MiniMax](https://x.com/MiniMax__AI), [Meta](https://x.com/AIatMeta) and [SpaceXAI](https://x.com/SpaceXSI). These are self-reported, so they mostly break ties.
- **The community.** Reddit, X, Hacker News and developer forums, used as a reality check, not as a score.

Four things worth knowing before you read on:

1. **Gemini 4 Carbon is on a waitlist.** Since its October 28 launch it's open only to vetted cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind Program, with no public API date. I rank it on capability, and every category names a fallback you can actually buy.
2. **There are two Muse Wonders.** The one in Meta's API is not the "Muse Wonder Full" in Meta's charts; Full only runs inside Muse Code plans ($50 a month and up). I say which one I mean.
3. **Claude's safety fallbacks.** Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 hand most flagged offensive-security requests to the older Opus 4.8, which drags down their cyber scores.
4. **Effort settings move scores.** The same model can swing 20+ points between "low" and "max" reasoning effort. I quote each model's best published setting.

## ☕ The Tea: what Reddit and X are saying

The crowd mostly agrees with the benchmarks on Opus 5.5 and disagrees loudly about GPT-6 Astra. Keep in mind these are user reports and opinions, not verified findings.

- **Claude Opus 5.5.** Builders on X praise it for front-end work and agentic coding, and a common split is "Opus for the frontend, Astra for the backend." The big gripe is the safety fallback: one developer doing defensive threat-intel work [saw most of a day's messages served by Opus 4.8](https://www.betterclaw.io/blog/claude-opus-5-5-agents-cost-rerouting) instead, and a [Cursor forum thread](https://forum.cursor.com/t/safeguard-fallback-to-opus-4-8-is-billed-as-the-model-you-selected-not-as-opus-4-8/172823) reports fallbacks billed at the price of the model you picked.
- **GPT-6 Astra.** Launch week in early September was full of wow moments (people rebuilt Manhattan in a game engine), then [complaints that it got "nerfed"](https://decrypt.co/378101/gpt-6-astra-openai-model-dumber-nerfed) flooded X. The team behind the Opencode agent went back to GPT-5.6 Sol, saying the bill doubled for little gain, and ChatGPT users on [OpenAI's forum](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-6-astra-is-a-disappointment-and-a-tool/1396406) complain it keeps pausing to ask for clarification.
- **Gemini 4 Carbon.** Benchmark cheers mixed with "another waitlist" fatigue; the Hacker News thread passed 800 points. Arena voters still pick Opus 5.5 over it in 69% of WebDev duels.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro.** r/LocalLLaMA is skeptical rather than hostile, with threads asking whether it beats the far cheaper DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. People were impressed Moonshot actually shipped 1.56 TB of weights on time.
- **Muse Wonder.** Hacker News boiled it down to the same price as Opus 5.5 with a lower score, and noticed that the Wonder you can buy through the API isn't the one in Meta's charts.
- **Grok 4.7.** Mixed. French tech site [Next](https://next.ink/257288/spacexai-lance-son-modele-grok-4-7-dont-les-performances-decoivent/) called its performance disappointing, and some users say Xiaomi's MiMo is better and cheaper.
- **Claude Sonnet 5.5.** Widely treated as the new daily driver: most of Opus's quality for half the price.

## The bottom line

If you can only pay for one family, Claude covers the most ground: Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 together reach the top 4 in 13 of the 15 categories. Legal drafting and cybersecurity are the two gaps.

A practical, budget-aware stack for a small business or agency:

- **Thinking and planning:** Claude Opus 5.5.
- **Everyday writing and coding:** Claude Sonnet 5.5.
- **Second opinion and code review:** GPT-6.1 Sol (a different lab catches different mistakes).
- **Bulk jobs (summaries, tagging, first drafts):** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or GLM-5.3 Flash.
- **Legal page drafts:** Muse Spark 1.3 or Muse Wonder, followed by a lawyer's review.

Two last tips. Run your own 10-prompt test before you switch anything, because benchmarks measure averages and your work isn't average. And check back in a month: when Gemini 4 Carbon opens to everyone, several of these rankings will shift.
